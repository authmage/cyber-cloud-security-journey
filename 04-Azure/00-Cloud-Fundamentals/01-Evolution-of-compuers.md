
# 💻 Evolution of Computers

Based on their development and size, two important initial categories of computers were:

1. **Mainframe Computers**
2. **Personal Computers (Desktop PCs)**

---

## 1️⃣ Mainframe Computers

- Large, powerful computers designed to process huge amounts of data and support thousands of users or transactions simultaneously.
- Occupy an entire room.
- Commonly used by banks, insurance companies, and government organizations.

**Example:** IBM zSeries Mainframes.

---

## 2️⃣ Personal Computers (PCs)

- Small computers designed for use by an individual, although they can also be shared.
- Used for everyday tasks.

**Examples:** Dell desktops, HP laptops, and Lenovo laptops.
