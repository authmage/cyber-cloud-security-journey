
# 💰 Types of Expenses

In cloud computing, expenses refer to the costs an organization pays to use cloud resources and services such as servers, storage, networking, databases, and applications.

---

## 1️⃣ CapEx – Capital Expenditure

- Buying physical equipment such as servers, routers, firewalls, etc.
- Requires upfront payments.
- Depreciation may apply over 3–5 years.
- **Overprovisioning:** Wastes capital by purchasing more resources than required.
- **Underprovisioning:** Can result in losing revenue due to insufficient resources.

---

## 2️⃣ OpEx – Operational Expenditure

- Money spent on regular business operations.
- Resources can be scaled easily and changed on demand.
- Recurring operational payments are common.
- The cloud provider owns and manages the underlying infrastructure.
