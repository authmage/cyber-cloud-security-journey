
# 🌐 Network

- Interconnection of **more than one node** forms a network.
- **Node:** Any device connected to a network, such as a PC, desktop, laptop, router, switch, printer, server, access point, firewall, or access controller.
- A **network** is a collection of two or more devices connected together so they can share information and resources.

---

## 📌 What Can a Network Share?

- **Files & Data:** Photos, documents, videos, or databases (e.g., Google Drive or a shared office folder).
- **Hardware:** One office printer or scanner can be shared by 20 computers.
- **Internet Connection:** One broadband router can provide internet access to every device in your house.
- **Apps & Software:** Multiple users can access the same business software stored on a central computer.

---

## 🎯 What Is the Need for a Network?

Without networks, you would have to copy files onto an external hard drive or pen drive and physically carry them from one computer to another.

### ❌ Without a Network

- **Time:** Transferring large amounts of data using external storage devices can take a lot of time.
- **Cost:** It can be expensive when computers are in different locations and large amounts of data require multiple external storage devices.
- **Security:**
  - If the external drive gets corrupted, the data may be lost.
  - Confidential data may be exposed while sharing it.
  - Data can be compromised, modified, or lost.

### ✅ With a Network

- **Less Time:** Data can be transferred quickly between connected devices.
- **Less Cost:** No external physical storage medium is required for every transfer. Data can be transferred through the network.
- **End-to-End Connectivity:** Devices can communicate directly through the network without needing to use external storage devices.
- **Secure Data Transfer:** Data can be protected during transmission using appropriate network security measures.

---

## 💡 Important Notes

- The internet is connected across countries and continents through **submarine fiber-optic cables** laid under the ocean.
- These cables use **light signals** to transmit data over long distances.
- **Speed of light in a vacuum:** \(3 \times 10^8\) m/s = approximately **300,000 km/s (3 lakh km/s)**.
